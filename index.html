01 Executive summary

This is a neutral research outline. Verify the project's purpose, users, revenue or fee model, and competitive position using primary sources before drawing conclusions.

02 Thesis to investigate

  • Does the protocol solve a specific problem better than alternatives?
  • Is usage sustained, measurable, and organic?
  • Does token demand connect to actual protocol activity?

03 Catalysts to monitor

  • Product releases and roadmap milestones confirmed by official channels.
  • Changes in users, fees, total value locked, or other relevant usage metrics.
  • Governance proposals, integrations, unlocks, and exchange or regulatory changes.

04 Risk checklist

High priority
Smart-contract, bridge, oracle, and admin-key risks — review audits and incident history.
Token
Supply schedule, insider allocations, unlock dates, liquidity, and concentration of ownership.
Adoption
Incentive-driven activity, concentrated usage, competitors, and dependence on a small number of integrations.

05 Evidence to collect

  • Official documentation and recent governance proposals.
  • Protocol analytics from a reputable data provider, with date ranges recorded.
  • Tokenomics documentation, unlock schedule, and circulating-supply methodology.
  • Independent security audits and disclosed incidents.

06 Open questions

What evidence would disprove the thesis? Which metrics are independently verifiable? What changed recently, and what is still unknown?
